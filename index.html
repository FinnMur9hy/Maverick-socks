FAQ

How do grip socks work?

Silicone pads inside the sock stop your foot sliding in the boot, and pads on the outside grip the boot’s footbed — less slip, sharper turns, better power transfer.

Can I wear them with any boots or trainers?

Yes — instead of or underneath your normal team sock, with football boots, futsal shoes, trainers and court shoes.

How do I wash them?

Machine wash cold at 30°C inside out, don’t iron the grips, air dry, and skip the fabric softener so the grips stay tacky.

Delivery and returns?

Flat-rate UK delivery, free over £30. Unworn socks in original packaging can be returned within 30 days. Full details show at checkout.
